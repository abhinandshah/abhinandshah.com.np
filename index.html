Flagship project

Spatial Wings WebGIS for Biratnagar Municipality

A five-module municipal GIS platform covering land, addresses, surveys, roads and live weather, built end to end in Flask and Leaflet.

  • 39,575parcels
  • 423.1 kmroads
  • 5modules
Problem
Land, road and address records lived in disconnected paper and desktop GIS files, with no shared, searchable system.
Data
Cadastral parcel sheets, municipal road survey data, drone and satellite imagery, and field-collected house points.
Build
Flask backend, Leaflet front end and a GeoJSON data layer, with admin authentication and a bilingual (EN/NP) interface.
Result
Parcels and roads are now filterable by ward in one browser-based system.
